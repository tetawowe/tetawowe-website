海潮漲退，帶來什麽？留下什麽？ 裝載地質記憶的沙礫、遠行后靠岸的漂流木，帶著鹹味的空氣和時間印記……
人潮來去，又在岸邊存放了什麽？ 一則寄不出去的心事、一份隨海岸綫無限延長的思念、一種無限遼闊的孤寂…… With the rise and fall of the tides, what is brought ashore? What is left behind? Grains of sand carrying geological memory, driftwood returning to shore after a long journey, air tinged with salt and the marks of time...
The crowds come and go. What do they leave at the water's edge? An unsent secret, a longing extended endlessly along the coastline, an immeasurable loneliness...

如果時間是用來丈量海的語言，岸邊的人又要用什麼方式去回應海的問候？ 《海的問候》以海岸為場域，透過書寫、停留與收集的裝置，建立人與海之間鬆散而持續的對話關係。這些裝置並不強調訊息的傳遞，而是讓語言、距離與時間得以被感知與安放。海既連結也阻隔，並以風、漂流物與變動的地景持續生成回應；人的回應不以抵達為目的，可以是書寫、凝視或停留。在這樣的往返之中，對話不一定被完成，卻在每一次感知與投放之間，形成一種微弱而真實的連結。 If time is a language for measuring the sea, how might those on shore respond to the sea's greeting? Sea Whisperer takes the coast as its field, using installations for writing, lingering, and collecting to establish a loose yet continuous dialogue between people and the sea. These installations do not seek to foreground the transmission of messages; instead, they make language, distance, and time perceptible and offer them a place to rest. The sea both connects and separates, continually composing replies through wind, drift matter, and shifting terrain. Human responses are not aimed at arrival; they may take the form of writing, gazing, or pausing. Within these exchanges, the dialogue need not be completed, yet between every act of sensing and setting forth, a faint but real connection takes shape.
[海的信箱 · echoes of the sea] 它是一個嵌入在岸邊，收集並記錄著時間和地質記憶的裝置 隨著潮汐開合，讓海水流經、停留、退去—— 在往復之間，它如沙漏、也如信箱， 將海潮帶來的漂流木、沙粒與石頭自然篩選與留下。 An installation embedded along the shore, gathering and recording memories of time and geology. As the tide opens and closes, seawater flows through, lingers, and recedes. In this continual cycle, it is both an hourglass and a mailbox, naturally sifting and retaining the driftwood, grains of sand, and stones brought in by the sea.
[信箋台 · in-between tides] 人與鳥共棲的一處暫停之地。候鳥沿著海岸線來去，在此停靠； 人們也在這裡，面向大海，留下片刻的停留與書寫。 等人來寫信，等鳥落腳，等信寄出， 也等大海，在某個時刻，輕輕回信。 A temporary resting place shared by people and birds. Migratory birds travel along the coastline and pause here; people, too, face the sea and allow themselves a moment to linger and write. Waiting for someone to write a letter, for a bird to land, for a letter to be sent, and for the sea, at some moment, to gently write back.
[彼人 · dear someone] 在海浪聲中，人們的思緒被無限放大，心事被人們投放進岸邊的信箱裏，被岸邊的信箱人守護著，並在某個時間點被寄出。 《彼人》信箱收集著人們寄給台灣本地/海外的信件，紅綠信箱分別為紅綠中華郵箱的意象。 In the sound of waves, thoughts are infinitely amplified. People cast their private thoughts into the mailbox by the shore, where they are held in the care of the mailbox keepers until, at some point in time, they are sent out. That Person mailbox collects letters addressed to recipients in Taiwan and overseas. Its red and green mailboxes draw on the imagery of Taiwan's familiar red and green postal boxes.
[彼岸 · dear some place] 《彼岸》信箱裝載著地理與空間距離的思念，它收集著人們寫給遠方的人的信件 一些能被抵達、或無法抵達的遠方。 The Other Shore mailbox carries the longing shaped by geographical and spatial distance. It gathers letters written to people far away, to places that may or may not be reached.
[彼時 · dear someday] 《彼時》信箱則訴説著時間距離。信件可以寫給過去或未來的自己，成爲一個岸邊的時間膠囊。 數年後，信件或許終於被取出，再展出，串起過去與現在的記憶，也讓每一段故事被重新閱讀與感受。 That Time mailbox speaks of temporal distance. Letters may be written to one's past or future self, becoming a time capsule at the shore. Years later, these letters may finally be retrieved and exhibited again, connecting memories of the past and present, and allowing each story to be read and felt anew.
