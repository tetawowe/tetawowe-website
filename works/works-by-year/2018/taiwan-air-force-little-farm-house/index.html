园丁小屋 一間屬於園丁的居所，包含苗圃、農具、茶居與種子庫四個功能各異的空間。當這四個空間組合在一起時，便如同一座完整的小房子，為園丁提供一個與種植相關的庇護所；而當它們拆卸分開時，各自獨立的空間同樣完善地承載了種植所需的各項設施。園丁小屋更像是坐落於土地之上、為協助種植活動而暫時存在的「工具」，可依需求拆卸分離或重新組合，也可隨著種植活動的轉移而移動，體現出建築與土地環境和諧共存的可能性。 A dwelling for gardeners, it comprises four spaces with distinct functions: a nursery, a tool shed, a tea room, and a seed bank. When these four spaces are combined, they form a complete little house that provides gardeners with a shelter centered around cultivation. When taken apart, each individual space still fully supports the facilities needed for planting activities. More than a fixed building, the little farm house is like a “tool” temporarily situated on the land to assist cultivation. It can be dismantled, separated, or reassembled according to different needs, and can also be relocated as planting activities shift, embodying the possibility of architecture coexisting harmoniously with the land and its environment.

在這場關於建築與土地和諧共存的探索中，我們所想像的園丁小屋，是一個可以四處移動、不受地理位置限制的種植基地。它由輕便的鐵框架與通透的鐵網牆體構成，便於移動與維修。當園丁小屋落腳後，只需將四個空間展開，便能形成一個完整的種植場域，並在周圍土地上進行全面耕作。當任務完成後，它又可重新組合在一起，前往下一個地點，不僅不會對土地留下傷害，反而以耕作與種植去「縫補」城市化所帶來的「缺口」。

In this exploration of how architecture can coexist harmoniously with the land, we envision the gardener’s hut as a mobile cultivation base that is not bound by any fixed location. It is composed of a lightweight steel frame and permeable steel mesh walls, making it easy to move and maintain. Once the gardener’s hut settles on a site, the four spaces can simply be unfolded to form a complete planting environment, allowing cultivation to take place fully across the surrounding land. When the task is complete, the spaces can be recombined and moved on to the next location, leaving no damage to the land. Instead, through farming and planting, it seeks to “stitch up” the “gaps” created by urbanization.

種子庫
第一個空間是園丁的種子庫（也是前廳），這也是種植的第一步驟。這裡用於儲存各種種子，顆粒狀或體積較小的種子可以裝在透明的壓克力盒子內，掛在牆體的鐵網上；體積較大或有堅硬外殼的種子則以袋子裝盛，懸掛在上方。 The first space is the farmer's seed pod, which also serves as the foyer. It marks the first step of cultivation. Various seeds are stored here: granular or smaller seeds can be placed in transparent acrylic boxes and hung on the metal mesh wall, while larger seeds or those with hard outer shells are kept in bags and suspended above.
