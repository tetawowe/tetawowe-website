負育群带聚落 空总文化实验室 來自熱帶的設計團隊首次來台創作，我們企圖尋找兩地共同關注的議題，比如環境、生態、城市／公共空間、永續經營等切身課題，讓這些普世價值結合建築設計，在空總作為台北市中心彌足珍貴的低密度建築量體與開放空間的大環境下，探討並衍生出各種建築、棲居與聚落的可能。 For this first creative work in Taiwan by a design team from the tropics, we sought to identify concerns shared by both places, such as the environment, ecology, the city / public space, and sustainable stewardship. By bringing these universal values into architectural design, the project explores and generates possibilities for architecture, dwelling, and settlement within the precious low-density building fabric and expansive open-space environment of the Taiwan Air Force site in the center of Taipei.

作為整個實驗建築的實構體現，窩工房（tetawowe）、共感地景（ArchiBlur Lab）與其他合作夥伴拓展出一系列負育建築／空間。其中，林中小屋系列之一的園丁小屋（Little Farm House）實構原型，將結合柏油路被開挖出來的場域，呈現農耕實驗建築，以及農耕內置於生活空間中的實質體驗；其他呈現在圖紙上的，還包括林中小屋系列之鳥居（Bird Stay），探索鳥類／昆蟲／大自然之間的關係；以及大型附屬建築體 Micro Forest，探討植物、文化實驗與空間共存的關係。

As a built manifestation of this experimental architectural project, tetawowe, ArchiBlur Lab, and other collaborators have developed a series of subtractive architectural and spatial interventions. Among them, the built prototype of Little Farm House from the Forest Cabin series is conceived in relation to the excavated asphalt ground, presenting an experiential integration of farming architecture and everyday living. Also represented in the drawings are Bird Stay from the Forest Cabin series, which explores the relationship between birds, insects, and the natural environment, as well as the larger ancillary structure Micro Forest, which investigates the coexistence of plants, cultural experimentation, and space.

園丁小屋 Little Farm House 園丁小屋坐落於土地之上，成為協助種植活動而暫時存在的「工具」。組合在一起時，它像是一個完整的小房子；而拆卸後的個別空間，同樣完善了種植所需要的各種設施。 Little Farm House sits directly on the land, serving as a temporary "tool" that supports planting activities. When assembled together, it reads as a complete little house; when disassembled, each individual space still provides the essential facilities needed for cultivation.

微森林 Micro Forest 想像空總大片的柏油路被樹木與濕地取代，成為適合魚、蟲、鳥類與小動物等生物進駐的環境。在這座微森林中，只要將原有的建築群稍作增刪、梳理與串聯，並配合適量的負育空間介面，我們即可發展出與自然生態平衡共生的建築載體，作為聚落。 Imagine the vast asphalt grounds of the Taiwan Air Force site replaced by trees and wetlands, forming an environment for fish, insects, birds, and other small animals. Within this Micro Forest, the existing building clusters are selectively edited, reorganized, and connected with subtractive spatial interfaces to create an architectural settlement that coexists in balance with nature.

鳥居 Bird Stay 當空總的微森林逐漸成形，並在濕地環境的配合下，預計將吸引各種停駐的鳥類。窩工房的提案，是在空總設立一個介於人類與鳥類之間的輕建築界面，提供觀察、研究、觀賞與學習的小平台。 As the micro forest at the Taiwan Air Force site gradually takes shape in tandem with its wetland environment, it is expected to attract a variety of visiting birds. tetawowe's proposal is to establish a lightweight architectural interface between humans and birds, creating a small platform for observation, research, appreciation, and learning.
