微森林 想像空總一大片的柏油路被樹木和濕地取代，適合魚、蟲、鳥類、小動物等生物的進駐。在這座微森林，只要將原有的建築群稍作增刪，梳理和串聯，配合酌量的負育空間介面，我們即可發展出和自然生態平衡共生的建築載體，作為聚落。這負育空間介面將扮演各種輔助的角色，包括提升縱橫動線、管線架構、半公共空間、農耕作業、再生能源站、觀察採集站、半戶外教學、表演舞台、臨時觀眾席等可以衍生無限作用無限想像的附屬性空間。負育空間介面是非永久性的 (non-permanent)，某些部份可以根據需要伸縮、組裝、移動，和動植物共享一片土地。 Imagine the vast asphalt grounds of the Air Force Headquarters being replaced by trees and wetlands, creating a habitat suitable for fish, insects, birds, and small animals. Within this micro forest, the existing building clusters need only slight additions and subtractions, together with reorganization and connection, to become architectural carriers that coexist in balance with the natural ecosystem and form a settlement. This negative-growth spatial interface plays many supporting roles, including enhancing horizontal and vertical circulation, utility networks, semi-public spaces, farming activities, renewable energy stations, observation and collection posts, semi-outdoor teaching areas, performance stages, and temporary spectator seating, as well as countless other auxiliary spaces open to infinite functions and imagination. The negative-growth spatial interface is non-permanent; some parts can expand, contract, be assembled, or be moved according to need, allowing humans, plants, and animals to share the same piece of land.
