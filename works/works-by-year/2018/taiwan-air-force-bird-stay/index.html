鸟居 當空總逐漸成形的微森林在濕地環境的配合下，預計會有各種停駐的鳥類。有些甚至是來自遠方的候鳥品種。鳥類是自然界生物鏈和生態繁殖的一部份。窩工房的提案是要在空總設立一個人類跟鳥類的輕建築界面，提供觀察、研究、觀賞和學習的小平台。鳥居設立於防空洞和兩顆榕樹之間，採取隱密的姿態。這個平台所取得的數據可以跟其他地區結合組成更完整的鳥類研究數據。 For this first creative work in Taiwan by a design team from the tropics, we sought to identify concerns shared by both places, such as the environment, ecology, the city / public space, and sustainable stewardship. By bringing these universal values into architectural design, the project explores and generates possibilities for architecture, dwelling, and settlement within the precious low-density building fabric and expansive open-space environment of the Taiwan Air Force site in the center of Taipei.
